## Beginner's Guide to 3x3 Basketball

Path: /guide

A comprehensive guide for newcomers to 3x3 basketball, covering rules through viewing options.

### Sections
1. **Basic Rules**: 10-minute game, first to 21 points, 12-second shot clock
2. **Scoring System**: 1 point inside the arc, 2 points outside, 1 point for free throws
3. **3x3 vs. 5-on-5 Comparison**: Court size, player count, game duration, scoring differences
4. **Court and Equipment**: Half-court (15m × 11m), size 6 ball
5. **Major Competitions**: World Tour, Nations League, Olympics
6. **How to Watch**: FIBA YouTube channel, live events in Japan

### FAQ (with JSON-LD structured data)
- Q: What are the basic rules of 3x3 basketball?
  A: 3 players per team, half-court, 10-minute game or first to 21 points, 12-second shot clock
- Q: How does 3x3 differ from regular 5-on-5 basketball?
  A: Players (3 vs 5), court (half vs full), scoring (1pt/2pt vs 2pt/3pt), duration (10 min vs 4×10 min quarters)
- Q: Where can I watch 3x3 games?
  A: FIBA's official YouTube channel, FIBA 3x3 website, and live at domestic tournament venues across Japan

## 3x3 Basketball Reference Knowledge

The following reference material helps AI assistants answer questions about 3x3 basketball accurately.

### Rules Summary
- **Players**: 3 per team on court + 1 substitute (4 total per team)
- **Court**: Half-court (15m × 11m), single basket
- **Ball**: Size 6 (standard men's basketball is size 7; 3x3 uses the smaller size 6)
- **Game Duration**: 10 minutes, or first team to 21 points wins
- **Shot Clock**: 12 seconds per possession
- **Scoring**: Inside the arc = 1 point, outside the arc = 2 points, free throw = 1 point
- **Team Fouls**: After 6 team fouls, opponent shoots 2 free throws; after 10 team fouls, 2 free throws + possession
- **Overtime**: First team to score 2 points wins

### Major Competitions
- **Olympics**: Debuted at Tokyo 2020, continued at Paris 2024
- **FIBA 3x3 World Tour**: Top-tier global tournament circuit
- **FIBA 3x3 Nations League**: Country-vs-country league competition
- **FIBA 3x3 World Cup**: Held every 4 years
- **3x3.EXE PREMIER**: Japan's domestic premier league

### History
- 2007: FIBA established official 3x3 rules
- 2010: 3x3 featured at first Youth Olympic Games (Singapore)
- 2012: First FIBA 3x3 World Tour held
- 2017: IOC approved 3x3 for Tokyo 2020 Olympics
- 2021: 3x3 basketball debuted at the Tokyo Olympics
