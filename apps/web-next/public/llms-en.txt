# 3x3 Observer's Hub

> A comprehensive Japanese-language portal for FIBA 3x3 basketball — covering tournament schedules, team profiles, FIBA world rankings, venue information, and news.

## About This Site

3x3 Observer's Hub is the go-to Japanese portal for FIBA 3x3 basketball information. It aggregates domestic and international event schedules, team data, world rankings, rules guides, venue details, and the latest news — all in Japanese.

The site primarily serves the Japanese 3x3 basketball community, including players, fans, team staff, and sports media.

## Content Overview

- [Events](/events): Domestic and international 3x3 tournament schedules and details
- [Teams](/teams): Profiles of 3x3 teams active in Japan (men's, women's, mixed)
- [FIBA Rankings](/rankings): FIBA 3x3 World Rankings — men's and women's TOP 20 with Japan highlight
- [Beginner's Guide](/guide): Rules, scoring, court specs, and how to watch 3x3 basketball
- [Venues](/venues): Tournament venue information
- [News](/news): Latest 3x3 basketball news with related team tags

## Key Facts About 3x3 Basketball

- 3-on-3 half-court game, governed by FIBA
- Olympic sport since Tokyo 2020
- 10-minute game or first to 21 points
- 12-second shot clock
- Scoring: 1 point inside the arc, 2 points outside the arc
- Ball: size 6 (smaller than standard men's size 7)
- Court: 15m x 11m half-court with one basket
